For plugins and templates

Open at
both ends

Your plugin’s relations go in. Yarn’s answers come out in Twig.

  • A source is a generator, so memory stays flat
  • Roll-up and site handling come with the context
  • usedBy() reads one table, cheap on a front end
EVENT_REGISTER_EDGE_SOURCES
Your own relations
A link field, a menu builder — anything that keeps references in its own table.
EVENT_DEFINE_FINDINGS
Your own checks
On the findings screen, in the console and in every export.
EVENT_AFTER_BUILD_GRAPH
Prune the graph
Trim it to what your site means by a relation, before it is cached.
